01 / STUDY AREA

A city-scale view

Stations were selected using the official City of Los Angeles boundary. Nearby independent cities are outside this study area, even when their postal addresses refer to the wider Los Angeles region.

02 / DATA & METHODS

Public, reported stations

The source was filtered to electric stations marked public and available, then spatially selected against the city boundary. Reported port counts determine the charging-level categories. Missing counts remain unknown.

03 / READ THE MAP

Stations and ports differ

Each point is a station record, which may contain multiple charging ports. Records at the same address are retained. Public stations may have parking charges, limited hours, or customer access conditions.
